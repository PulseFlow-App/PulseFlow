What this role can do

The company account is the control center for the entire operation.

Properties

Manage every company property, update property details, assign people to properties, and keep the portfolio organised. Add house guides, notes, and important information for each property.

People and access

Manage the organisation, invite managers, field staff and guests, assign specific properties to specific people, and control who can access what. Search and choose staff by skills, location, map and reviews. Keep the team connected with chat, mentions, weekly endorsements and a company leaderboard.

Operations

Create, assign, track and close company-wide tasks. Oversee jobs, service orders and vendors, with direct access to vendors by call, WhatsApp or LINE. Confirm service orders and keep track of everything happening across the portfolio.

Money

Review and approve bills, track company-wide spending, and manage guest deposits including deductions with photo evidence. Work across 20 currencies with automatic conversion.

Stay informed and in control

Get in-app notifications and phone push notifications, generate reports and exports for individual categories or the whole company, and track referrals and team invitations.

Planned features

  • Google Calendar sync connect company and property calendars so bookings, tasks and important dates stay in sync.
  • Recurring tasks automatically create routine jobs such as cleaning, inspections and maintenance.
  • Approval flows set who needs to approve purchases, expenses and service orders.
  • Property performance see revenue, costs, occupancy, maintenance and operational activity per property.
  • Automated reminders deadlines, upcoming jobs, expiring documents, unpaid bills and scheduled maintenance.
  • Documents and contracts store leases, invoices, warranties, contracts and property documents in one place.
  • Maintenance history keep a complete record of repairs, costs, vendors and recurring issues for each property.
  • Smart scheduling coordinate staff, vendors and tasks around bookings and availability.
  • Advanced company analytics compare properties, teams, costs and operational performance over time.
  • Accounting integrations connect external accounting or bookkeeping tools to reduce manual financial work.
