01 · Overview

One screen, the whole picture

Occupancy, urgent tasks, and upcoming check-ins are updated live by your on-site manager. Know what needs attention without opening a chat.

02 · Properties

Every property, status, and stay dates

Company inventory first, then your personal list. Each card shows occupancy, check-in and check-out, and location—so you and your manager share the same picture without a spreadsheet.

03 · Tasks

Every task has an owner

Every task has a property, deadline, and responsible person. Filter by Mine or Urgent to see what needs attention now.

04 · Work orders

Send clear jobs. Track them.

Send the job details: what, where, and when. Contact anyone by call, WhatsApp, or LINE, and see when they were contacted.

05 · Confirmations

Know what was seen and accepted

Staff confirm each job with Read & Agreed before it becomes assigned. Bills and job confirmations stay logged in Notifications.

06 · Finances

See what you spend

Filter by period, property, or category. See total, paid, and pending amounts. Approve bills in one tap.

07 · Performance

Know who delivers

Rate staff and service providers weekly. Build a record of performance and see who to keep, renew, or replace.
