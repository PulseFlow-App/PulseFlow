Know your properties are running right, without picking up the phone.

Most owners only hear about a problem after a guest complains or a bill shows up with no explanation. Pulse Flow Ops closes that gap. Occupancy, urgent work, and spend, all visible the moment you open the app.

01 · The whole day

The full state of your operation in one screen

Occupancy, urgent tasks, and this week's check-ins are all on Home, updated live by your on-site manager. You do not need to read a chat thread to know if today is under control.

02 · Scheduled work

Every task has a property, a time, and an owner

Nothing gets assigned verbally and forgotten. Filter by Mine or Urgent to see exactly what needs attention right now.

03 · Vendors

Vendor requests sent as structured orders, not messages

Linked vendors receive the full job detail: what, where, when. Outside contacts are one tap away by call, WhatsApp, or LINE. You always know a vendor was actually contacted.

04 · Paper trail

Confirmation that staff saw and accepted the job

Every service order posts to team chat and requires a Read and agreed tap before it counts as assigned. Bill submissions and job confirmations are logged separately in Notifications.

05 · Money

Know your spend without asking for a spreadsheet

Filter by period, property, or category. Total, paid, and pending amounts are always visible. Approve a pending bill with one tap.

06 · Reputation

A record of who is reliable, not a guess

Rate staff and vendors weekly. Ratings build a leaderboard so you know who to keep, renew, or replace, based on data, not memory.
