Why Pulse

A pulse is the first vital sign checked because it tells you instantly whether something needs attention. Pulse Flow Ops gives you one honest reading per villa: occupied or empty, paid or pending, on track or urgent. Not a spreadsheet of everything.

01 · Live status

See each villa's state as your manager updates it

Occupied, turnover, available, and maintenance show on every property card when your on-site manager changes status. You open the app and know which villa needs attention without a status call.

02 · Bill approval

Approve expenses with the villa and reason attached

Staff submit bills with the property name, category, and description filled in. You review pending totals, filter by villa or period, and tap Mark paid when the charge is right.

03 · Urgent work

Urgent tasks sit on your home screen, not in chat history

When a turnover, repair, or vendor visit cannot wait, it shows on your dashboard with the villa name and due date. You see it the moment you open the app.

04 · Team chat

One ops thread with your manager, not three apps

Company workspaces include a single team chat for your ops group. Bookings, handoffs, and day-of updates stay in one place instead of scattered across WhatsApp, LINE, and email threads.
