What this role can do

You have the same operational access as the owner. The organisation's own details, the company property list and property access for staff stay with the owner.

Properties

See every company property with its status, dates and details, and keep the house guide, notes and important information up to date on each one.

People and access

Invite staff, guests and colleagues, and find new people through staff search by skills, area, map and reviews. Keep the team connected with chat, mentions, weekly endorsements and a company leaderboard.

Operations

Create, assign, track and close company-wide tasks, with filters for what is yours and what is urgent. Send jobs and service orders that people confirm with a tap, reach vendors by call, WhatsApp or LINE, and handle guests: deposits, deductions, briefings and date requests.

Money

Review and approve bills, filter and total the company's finances and mark what has been paid. Work across 20 currencies with automatic conversion. Billing for the account itself stays with the owner.

Stay informed and in control

Get in-app notifications and phone push, and pull reports and exports for a single category or the whole company, so the weekly handover is already written.

Connect your agent

Create a connection key in Settings and paste it into the agent's shell. The agent can read the team, properties, tasks, jobs and bills, create tasks and jobs, and mark a task done. It never gets the database key. Disconnect anytime.

Planned features

  • Google Calendar sync connect company and property calendars so bookings, tasks and important dates stay in sync.
  • Recurring tasks automatically create routine jobs such as cleaning, inspections and maintenance.
  • Approval flows set who needs to approve purchases, expenses and service orders.
  • Property performance see revenue, costs, occupancy, maintenance and operational activity per property.
  • Automated reminders deadlines, upcoming jobs, expiring documents, unpaid bills and scheduled maintenance.
  • Documents and contracts store leases, invoices, warranties, contracts and property documents in one place.
  • Maintenance history keep a complete record of repairs, costs, vendors and recurring issues for each property.
  • Smart scheduling coordinate staff, vendors and tasks around bookings and availability.
  • Advanced company analytics compare properties, teams, costs and operational performance over time.
  • Accounting integrations connect external accounting or bookkeeping tools to reduce manual financial work.
