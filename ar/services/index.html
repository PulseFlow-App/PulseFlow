FAQ

Do I pay through Pulse Flow?

No. You pay the professional directly. Pulse Flow connects you and does not take the payment.

Is the app on the App Store?

Not yet. Open services.pulseflow.site in Safari or Chrome and add it to your Home Screen. Store listings come later.

Can I list my trade for free?

Yes. Create a free listing with your trade, area, and languages. People nearby can find you and message you.

Which languages work?

English, Russian, and Thai are supported for seekers and professionals. You write in your language; they read in theirs when the listing supports it.

How do I reach a pro?

Open their profile and message them on WhatsApp or LINE, or in the app when both of you are there.
