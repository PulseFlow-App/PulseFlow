Roles

Cleaning and other job types are work labels, not separate account roles. Field teammates use the staff seat.

  • Owner: company settings, properties, invites and software billing when live.
  • Manager: day-to-day ops with the owner. Some company settings stay with the owner.
  • Staff: assigned properties, tasks, jobs, team chat and expense bills.
  • Guest: stay tools, house guide, Support with the host and deposit status. No team chat or master ops files.
  • AI agent: optional helper for owners and managers only, scoped to their workspace.

Money
