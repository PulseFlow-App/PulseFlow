Every task has a property, deadline, and responsible person. Filter by Mine or Urgent to see what needs attention now.

06 · Finances

Keep bills moving on site

See pending totals, mark expenses paid, and keep owner and staff reimbursements in one list — without digging through chat photos for the last receipt.

Know who delivers

Rate staff and service providers weekly. Build a record of performance and see who to keep, renew, or replace.
