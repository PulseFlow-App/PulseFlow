01 · What's hot today

Ops readiness, occupancy, turnovers, and urgent tasks sit on one Home screen. Scroll for check-ins and this week's task pulse, without rebuilding the plan in WhatsApp.

02 · Every villa, one view

Company inventory first, then your personal list. Each card shows occupancy, check-in and check-out, and location, so you and the owner share the same data, no spreadsheet.

03 · A name on every task

Every task has a property, deadline, and responsible person. Filter by Mine or Urgent to see what needs attention now.

04 · Clear jobs, tracked

Send the job details: what, where, and when. Contact anyone by call, WhatsApp, or LINE, and see when they were contacted.

05 · Seen and accepted

Staff confirm each job with one tap before it becomes assigned. Bills and job confirmations stay logged in Notifications.

06 · Bills keep moving

See pending totals, mark expenses paid, and keep owner and staff reimbursements in one list, without digging through chat photos for the last receipt.

07 · Who delivers

Rate staff and service providers weekly. Build a record of performance and see who to keep, renew, or replace.
