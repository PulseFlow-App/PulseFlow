01 · The morning brief

See what needs a handoff today

Occupancy, urgent tasks, and upcoming check-ins sit on one Home screen. Start the day knowing which properties are hot and which jobs still need confirmation.

02 · Clear ownership

Assign work that cannot disappear in a thread

Every task carries a property, a deadline, and a person. Filter by urgent or assignee so nothing stays verbal, and nothing gets lost between morning standup and evening wrap-up.

03 · Book the job

Turn vendor requests into trackable orders

Send what, where, and when in one order. Linked vendors get the job in-app. Outside contacts stay one tap away on call, WhatsApp, or LINE. You keep a record of every outreach.

04 · Proof of contact

Know the job was seen before you escalate

Service orders post to team chat and need Read & Agreed before they count as assigned. When staff finish, owners and managers get notified - and the completion lands in chat too.

05 · Spend on site

Keep bills moving without a spreadsheet chase

Staff submit receipts. You and the owner see pending totals by property and category. Mark paid when it clears - no more digging through photo albums for the last receipt.

06 · Team signal

Surface who is reliable before the next hire

Weekly endorsements and the leaderboard give owners a clear view of performance. As the manager, you run the ops that make those ratings fair - real jobs, real confirmations, real outcomes.
