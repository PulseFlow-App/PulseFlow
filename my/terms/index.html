What Pulse Flow is

Pulse Flow is a shared ops tool: properties, tasks, jobs, expense bills, contacts, team chat, guest stays, house guides, handoff notes, property handoff between personal and company workspaces, maintenance logs, reports, optional channel calendar sync (Airbnb, Booking.com, Guesty) and an optional AI agent. It is not a party to anyone's rental or employment relationship.

  • We are not a landlord, booking marketplace, escrow, bank, payment processor for stays, or employer of your staff.

  • Demo seats are for exploration only. Demo content is sample data you cannot edit into your own workspace, and we may reset it at any time. Use a real account for your properties and team.
