Properties

Simply add your property maintenance staff contacts and book them with a call or message. Or invite them to the app for both sides' convenience - staff will be able to accept jobs in-app.

Money

Guests

FAQ

My cleaners are not going to use another app.

They do not need to learn anything new. You send them a link, they open it in their browser, and it sits on their phone like any other app. No download, no account, no App Store. They see only their jobs, in their own language, and confirm with one tap. If they can read a WhatsApp message, they can use this.

My manager already has a system. Why would they switch?

They would not be switching - they would be adding one place where the jobs they already manage become visible to everyone else. The chat stays. What changes is that a job now has a confirmed status, a cost attached to it, and a record that does not disappear when someone leaves.

Everything is on Airbnb and Booking. Does this replace them?

No. Bookings stay where they are. Pulse Flow is about what happens after the booking: who cleans, what broke, what it cost, and what the guest can see. Importing from Google Calendar is planned. There is no direct sync with Airbnb or Booking today.

What does it cost?

A personal workspace is free forever. For a company, only the owner pays - team seats are free however many people you add. Billing is not live yet, so every account gets 3 months of full access from the day it signs up.

Will my guests have to do anything complicated?

No. You send them a link. They open it, read the house rules, and see their deposit balance. No download, no account, no payment through the app. Payments go directly to you - the app just keeps a record of the amount, any deductions, and the balance so both sides see the same thing.

Can a guest dispute a deposit deduction?

Every deduction is recorded in the app with a reason and a date. Both you and the guest see the same balance. There is no "I never received the rules" or "I don't remember agreeing to that" - it is all in one place, timestamped, and visible to both sides.

What if a guest wants to book again?

Returning guests can send a date request directly through the app. It goes to the owner or manager without going through your personal number or a third-party platform.

What happens when a staff member leaves?

Their job history, vendor contacts, and property notes stay in the system. Nothing walks out the door with their phone. The next person you bring in sees the same information from day one.

Can I use this for one property?

Yes. The personal plan is free forever and covers one or two properties you manage yourself. You get the same features - jobs, expenses, guests - without needing a team.

Is my data private?

Each workspace is separate. Your properties, team, guests, and financials are visible only to the people you invite. Guests see only what you share with them.
