FAQ

My cleaners are not going to use another app.

They do not need to learn anything new. You send them a link, they open it in their browser, and it sits on their phone like any other app. No download, no account, no App Store. They see only their jobs, in their own language, and confirm with one tap. If they can read a WhatsApp message, they can use this.

Everything is on Airbnb and Booking. Does this replace them?

No. Bookings stay where they are. Pulse Flow is about what happens after the booking: who cleans, what broke, what it cost, and what the guest can see. Paste an Airbnb listing link to Quick fill property details. Paste each listing's private calendar export (.ics) from Airbnb, Booking.com or Guesty into a property to import reserved nights into the Bookings calendar. Owners and managers can also connect Guesty Open API once in Settings to import listings and calendars. That does not replace those platforms' listings or checkout.

What does it cost?

Personal is free forever. Company is USD 15 / month and includes 10 team seats; each extra seat is USD 2 / month. Pro is USD 24 / month for Connect your agent. Right now billing is not live, so every account gets 3 months of full access, including Pro, from the day it signs up.

Can I use this for one property?

Yes. Personal is free forever for properties on your own list. Jobs, expenses and guests work without a team. When a real owner joins, invite them to take those properties into their company; you can stay as manager.

Is my data private?

Each company and personal workspace is separate. Properties, team, guests and financials are visible only to people in that workspace. Guests see only what you share with them. A manager on several companies switches workspace in Settings; that does not mix inventories. Guesty Client ID and Client secret are encrypted; readable secrets are never stored in our databases. Calendar export links stay private to your company.
