1 · Whiteboard

Music on. Users should feel energetic and serious at once — like a clear morning over the villa list: ready to act, not to decorate.

Energetic

Orange action. Amber signals. Motion with purpose.

Serious

Deep ink. Direct Hebrew/English. No toy UI.

Airy

Cool white canvas. Sky washes. Room to breathe.

2 · Principles

Design is ruthless elimination. If you try to do everything, you do nothing.

Do

  • One job per section
  • Hero: brand + H1 + line + CTA + property visual
  • Mobile first, RTL-ready
  • Tokens only — no random hex

Cut

  • Stat strips and badge piles in the first viewport
  • Warm cream + terracotta clichés
  • Purple SaaS gradients
  • Decorative cards that aren’t interactive

5 · Primary visuals

Property-connected. Breathing horizon. Hero that stands out — not a dashboard collage.

Property on the horizon Real villa photography + slow sky breath. No abstract blobs.

Must show

  • Villa / pool / deck / work-on-site
  • Job cards with property photo
  • Status you can trust at a glance

Modern, not everywhere yet

  • Living horizon atmosphere (CSS breath)
  • Cool canvas instead of warm cream SaaS
  • Selective micro-motion, not scroll circus

6 · Israeli audience — why they walk the platform

Design language speaks to motives, not decoration. Make every screen answer one of these.

Remote control

See the day without calling the manager. Occupancy and fire from afar.

Money trail

Every cost lands on a property. Approve. Export. No lost receipts.

Staff reliability

Job accepted in-app — not buried in WhatsApp. Status you can show a partner.

Language & trust

Write once; team reads Hebrew / Thai / English. Serious tool, not a toy.

7 · Design language — Horizon

Soul of the brand (language) + body of the system (tokens & components). Consistency across site and app.

Voice

  • Direct, short, outcome-first
  • Serious energy — never cute
  • Chat stays; ops leave the chat

Patterns

  • Pill CTAs in action orange
  • Soft sky panels for agent / trust
  • Phone frames with real screenshots

Do

Reuse Horizon tokens. Match this brief. Prefer elimination.

Don’t

Invent “Primary Soft Alt 2.” Ship purple glow. Warm-cream reopen.

9 · AI & implementation guards

From vibe-coded UI discipline: tokens → components → no drift. Agents open DESIGN.md first.

Trends we adopt

  • Mobile-first, cut-to-the-chase heroes
  • Meaningful micro-interactions
  • Airy, ethical contrast & performance
  • Selective motion — not maximalism

Trends we skip

  • VR / AR for marketing
  • Brutalist / maximalist overload
  • Dynamic cursor gimmicks as default
  • Purple-on-white AI defaults
