## 1. What we are about

**Pulse Flow** is property operations software for vacation rentals.

**Main phrase:**  
**The pulse of your rental operations**

Supporting line / mission:  
*Property management without the chat chaos: properties, jobs, tasks, vendors, bills and guests in one workspace. Everyone sees their own part, in their own language.*

Owners, managers, cleaners, vendors and guests currently run villas on WhatsApp chaos: “who took the job?”, “which villa?”, “where’s the receipt?”, “is Coral done?”. Pulse Flow replaces that mess with a shared workspace:

| In the app | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Properties | One list with status, map, stay dates, photo |
| Jobs & vendors | Assign work, staff accept in-app, confirmed status |
| Money | Bills and receipts tied to a property — money trail |
| Guests | House rules, deposit, date requests without personal numbers |
| Languages | Write once; team reads in their own language |

**We are not** another Airbnb / Booking clone. Bookings stay where they are. We own **what happens after the booking**: who cleans, what broke, what it cost, and what the guest can see.

**Site / product:** [https://www.pulseflow.site](https://www.pulseflow.site)  
**App today:** web app — open from the site (“Open app” / Start free); works on phone like an app, no App Store required  
**Support:** support@pulseflow.site  

**Apple App Store:** we have plans to publish there, but timing is **not fixed** — it depends on project budget. Do not promise a store date in meetings; say the product is live on the web now, and native store listing is a later goal.

**Launch focus:** Koh Phangan, Thailand — onboarding the first teams by hand.

## 2. Who the audience is (who you talk to)

### Primary (pay / decide)
- **Property owners** — often Israeli or foreign owners running villas remotely; they want control, a money trail, and staff who actually confirm work.
- **Local operators / villa managers** — the person who builds the day and gets blamed when chat fails.

### Secondary (use daily, free seats)
- **Field staff** — cleaners, maintenance, vendors (accept jobs, send receipts from the phone).
- **Guests** — invited stay guests (rules, deposit, rebook requests).

### On Phangan, prioritise
1. Small–mid villa operators and management companies (1–15+ properties).  
2. Owners who already feel WhatsApp pain.  
3. Managers who can bring an owner along (or decide themselves).  

**Pitch angle that works:**  
“You keep WhatsApp for chat. Ops leave the chat — jobs confirmed, receipts on the property, owner sees Done without asking.”
