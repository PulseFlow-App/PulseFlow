# Pulse Flow · Koh Phangan: BD brief

For the local marketing and BD specialist on Koh Phangan.

**Tone:** energetic and serious. In control. Short verbs.

---

## The speech

### 1. Why it exists

Pulse Flow started as a tool for personal use, built and advised by someone working in property operations. Every piece answers one real need from the field: how the team talks, how the team coordinates, inside the company, not around it.

### 2. The problem

First, where are you with the team and the day-to-day? Are you still learning the people and the processes, or is the operation already running? Do you use any tools that keep jobs, costs, and statuses clear, or does most of it still live in chat?

Small property teams almost always start in WhatsApp or Telegram. That works for a while. It does not scale.

Think of the last job that went out. Who took it? How does the owner see what was done, where, and for how much? In a group chat, they usually don’t. The cleaner says “done.” Maybe. The receipt is a photo on someone’s phone. At 9 pm the owner writes: “Is Coral Villa finished, and what did it cost?” Someone scrolls for twenty minutes and answers from memory.

That is not a system. That is the business living in a chat log.

*[If speaking to a manager, add:]* And when chat fails, you take the blame. This makes your day provable to the owner.

If this is where you are now, here is what we offer.

### 3. How we fix it

Pulse Flow is one company workspace where each role has its own place to work.

The owner invites a manager; together they bring staff and guests into the same account. Company is USD 15 / month with 10 team seats included; extra seats are USD 2 / month. Stay guests do not count. You add properties to the list and the day can start. Property details can be filled fast from a PDF, an Airbnb link, or text pasted from elsewhere.

Jobs are sent as requests staff accept or decline, so you know who took the work. When a job is confirmed, the assignee can add it to Google Calendar or Apple Calendar, so the appointment sits next to the rest of their week. Tasks stay in one list with assignees and urgency, and show up on the home dashboard as focus of the day and what got closed.

Stay nights sync the other way: paste each listing’s private calendar export (`.ics`) from Airbnb or Booking.com onto the property, and reserved check-in and check-out dates land in Pulse’s Bookings calendar for occupancy and planning.

Receipts and bills live on the property in the app. Maintenance, money, and occupancy can be exported for reports.

Guests get their own layer: date and price requests for a place they already love, house instructions, deposit, and messages, in a clear trail instead of informal “friendly discount” chats.

Languages and currency travel with the team: you write in English or Hebrew, they read in Thai, or the reverse.

*[If they already run a PMS / channels:]* Bookings and channels stay where they are. We are what happens after the booking. We do not replace Airbnb or Booking.

**If they ask about the AI agent**

Connect your agent is part of Pro (+USD 9 / month on Personal or Company; Company itself is USD 15 / month when billing is live). For the founding ten companies on Phangan, this layer is included free for now.

In-app agent chat is only the first step. Connect your agent is a personal key from Settings that plugs Pulse into an AI client you already use (for example Cursor, Claude Desktop, or another MCP-capable agent). The agent works in your live workspace through tools: properties, team, tasks, jobs, bills. It never gets the database key. You can disconnect anytime.

Ops do not have to start inside Pulse’s screens. You can stay in the agent you already open for other work and still:

- Morning brief: “what’s open today / what’s urgent before check-in” while you are already in that agent for mail, notes, or planning  
- Messy input → structured work: paste a WhatsApp dump, a vendor quote, or a checklist and create the task or job on the right villa and person  
- Same session as spreadsheets or drafts: list bills, book a job, or mark a task done so status lives in ops, not another chat  
- Owner or manager remote control from a laptop agent, with the same confirmations the team sees  

We do not replace Airbnb, Booking, or your accounting package. The agent makes Pulse the ops layer those tools feed into, through the AI workflow you already trust.

### 5. Why use us at all

You get a clear view from wherever you are: Tel Aviv, a beach in Srithanu, or on site. Occupancy, spend, and who took which job stay in one place. Owners see Done without chasing. Managers can show how the day ran. Staff see only their work. Guests use the app instead of calling for every detail. That helps every side and raises productivity.

**Close (every time):**  
Two minutes. I’ll show you the same job going out and coming back. Then you tell me where this wouldn’t fit how you run your week.
