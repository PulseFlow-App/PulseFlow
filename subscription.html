Plans

Three tiers. Clear seats for each villa role.

Free covers personal ops and day-to-day field work. Basic unlocks manager reporting tools. Full unlocks the company workspace for the owner who pays for the team.

Trials

Try before you pay

  • Company owners (Full) New company workspaces get a 30-day Full trial. Invite managers and staff, assign villas, use team chat, book jobs, and run owner finances during the trial. After it ends, the owner subscribes to keep those company writes open.
  • Managers (Basic) Managers who upgrade for reporting tools also get a 30-day Basic trial. Exports, printable weekly summaries, and extended job history stay available for that window, then Basic renews separately from the owner’s Full plan.

Personal workspaces stay Free forever. Field staff and cleaners sit on Free seats inside a company; they do not get billed personally.

Subscription tiers

Tier 1

Free

Personal use, plus company managers and staff on limited seats.

  • Personal workspace Solo villas, tasks, bills, and a simple phone book. No team invites, no company chat, no in-app job booking.
  • Company staff / cleaner Assigned villas, jobs with Read & agreed, bill submit, contacts, and team chat. No company-wide invites or villa assignment controls.
  • Company manager (limited) Day-to-day ops on assigned villas: status updates, tasks, contacts, bills, chat, and booking jobs when the company is on Full. No CSV/PDF exports, no printable weekly ops pack, no extended archive downloads.
Start free

Tier 2

Basic

For company managers who need report-ready outputs for owners and partners.

  • Everything on Free manager seats Live villa status, tasks, contacts, bills, chat, and job booking (when the company Full plan is active).
  • Exports for reports Download bills, tasks, and villa status as CSV/spreadsheet for presentations, owner updates, and accounting handoffs.
  • Printable weekly ops summary One-page week view of turnovers, urgent work, and pending bills ready to share outside the app.
  • Extended job & bill history Pull longer date ranges and filter archives by villa, category, and period before you export.
  • Handoff snapshots Save a dated villa status snapshot for shift changes or contractor briefings.

Basic is a manager add-on. It does not replace the owner’s Full company subscription for invites, company villas, and team tooling.

Start Basic trial

Tier 3

Full

For company owners who run the shared ops pulse for the whole team.

  • Company workspace Invite managers and staff with role + job title (link or QR), assign villas, and keep one live inventory.
  • Team ops In-app team chat, in-app service orders with Read & agreed, weekly endorsements, and the company leaderboard.
  • Owner finances Spend by villa, period, and category; approve pending bills with villa and reason attached.
  • Manager Basic seats included Managers on your company get Basic reporting tools while Full is active, so they can export without a separate seat charge during the owner subscription.
  • Billing portal Owner manages Stripe subscription, invoices, and renewal from Settings.
