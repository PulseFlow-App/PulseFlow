Privacy Policy

Pulse Flow Ops · Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulse Flow Ops collects, uses, and shares information when you use pulseflow.site, app.pulseflow.site, and related services (the “Service”).

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: name, email, role, organization, and authentication identifiers.
  • Operations data: properties, tasks, contacts, messages, service orders, bills, endorsements, notifications, and related metadata.
  • Files: property photos and receipt images you upload.
  • Billing data: Stripe processes card details for company subscriptions. We store Stripe customer and subscription IDs, not full card numbers.
  • Technical data: device/browser type, IP address, and basic usage logs needed to secure and operate the Service.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and improve rental operations features
  • Authenticate users and enforce organization roles
  • Send in-app notifications (jobs, chat, bills)
  • Process company subscriptions and prevent abuse
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share data only as needed with:

  • Infrastructure providers (hosting, database, storage, auth) that process data on our instructions
  • Stripe for company payments
  • Your organization: teammates you invite can see data according to their role
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect the Service and users

4. Retention

We retain account and operations data while your organization is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account; some records may remain where required by law (for example billing history).

5. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; use strong passwords and limit who you invite.

6. Your choices

  • Update profile and organization settings in the app
  • Manage or cancel company billing via the billing portal
  • Contact us to access, correct, or delete personal data where applicable

7. International use

The Service may be hosted in regions chosen by our providers. If you use Pulse Flow Ops from another country, your information may be processed outside your home jurisdiction.

8. Children

The Service is for business operations and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy and will post the new version with a revised date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@pulseflow.site. See also our Terms of Service.
