Privacy Policy

Pulse Flow · Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulse Flow collects, uses, and shares information when you use pulseflow.site, app.pulseflow.site, and related services (the “Service”).

Summary

  • We collect account, operations, and technical data needed to run the Service.
  • We use data to authenticate you, deliver features, and send notifications.
  • We share data with infrastructure providers, Stripe when billing is enabled, and teammates according to role.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

By role

Stay guests

  • We store date requests, quotes, stays, deposit status, briefings, and Support chat (including /deposit and /cancel).
  • Receipt images in Support are stored in cloud storage.
  • Your host and their team can see booking and Support data for your stay.

Owners & managers

  • We process property inventory, tasks, contacts, team chat, bills, reports, guest stays, and billing metadata for your organization.
  • Guest Support messages and deposit records are visible to authorized owners and managers.
  • Stripe customer and subscription IDs are stored on the organization; card numbers are handled by Stripe.

Cleaning team

  • We store your profile, assigned villas, job confirmations, and submitted bills.
  • Receipt photos for expenses are stored in cloud storage.
  • Guest Support chat is separate unless you are also invited as a guest.

Field staff

  • We store tasks, jobs, team chat, endorsements, and submitted bills.
  • Optional talent directory listings may be visible to owners and managers searching in the app.
  • Guest stay data remains limited to hosts and that guest.

Retention & security

We retain data while your organization is active and for a reasonable period afterward. We use encrypted transport and access controls.

Your choices

  • Update profile and organization settings in the app
  • Manage or cancel company billing via the billing portal when available
  • Contact us to access, correct, or delete personal data where applicable

International use & children

Data may be processed outside your home country. The Service is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We may update this Policy and post a revised date. Continued use means you accept the updated Policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@pulseflow.site. See also our Terms of Service.
