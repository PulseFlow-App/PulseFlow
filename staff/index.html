03 · Read & Agreed

New service orders wait for your Read & Agreed. That is how owners and managers know you were reached - and how you protect yourself when the brief changes mid-day.

Weekly stars from owners build a shareable reputation page. That record travels with you - useful when you want the next contract or a stronger seat on the team.
